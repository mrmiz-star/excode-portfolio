# MR MIZ — Portfolio & EXCODE Corporation

A modern, high-converting portfolio landing page for **MR MIZ** built under **EXCODE Corporation**. Optimized for ad traffic, direct client conversions, and highlighting custom software development capabilities.

![MR MIZ Portfolio](/public/images/pyget_x.png)

---

## ⚡ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React 19 + Vite 8
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 + Vanilla CSS Design System
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Graphics & Canvas**: Custom Living Digital Background (Canvas 2D particle matrix, grid animation, mouse reactivity)
- **Typography**: Google Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit, JetBrains Mono)

---

## 🛠️ Section Architecture

1. **Header**: Sticky glassmorphic navbar with logo, nav links (`Home`, `Services`, `Projects`, `About`, `EXCODE`, `FAQ`, `Contact`), mobile drawer, and CTA (`Start a Project →`).
2. **Hero**: Headline *"I Build Digital Solutions That Move Businesses Forward."*, subtitle, CTAs, trust line, living digital canvas background with subtle EXCODE watermark.
3. **Quick Trust Strip**: Glass banner featuring *Custom Solutions*, *Modern Technology*, *Direct Communication*, and *Local & International*.
4. **About MR MIZ**: Clear narrative (*"Turning Ideas Into Working Software."*) and core discipline cards.
5. **Services**: 6 service cards (*Web Development*, *Desktop Applications*, *AI & Machine Learning*, *Automation*, *E-Commerce*, *Custom Software*) with examples and CTAs.
6. **Featured Projects**: Screenshots, tech tags, descriptions, and interactive detail modals for:
   - **PyGet X** (Python, PySide6, FFmpeg)
   - **EXCODE PDF Tool** (Python, PySide6, PDF)
   - **Shop Management System** (Flask, Python, SQLite)
   - **Discord Management System** (Python, Discord API)
7. **How I Work**: 4-step process (*01 Tell Me Your Idea*, *02 Plan*, *03 Build*, *04 Launch*).
8. **EXCODE Corporation**: Clear brand ecosystem positioning (*MR MIZ = Developer*, *EXCODE Corporation = Technology Brand*).
9. **Why Work With Me?**: 6 value pillars (*Custom-Built*, *Modern & Professional*, *Direct Communication*, *Practical Solutions*, *Flexible*, *Ongoing Support*).
10. **Homepage FAQ**: 6 high-intent accordions + `View All 20 FAQs` modal with live search & filters.
11. **Final CTA**: High conversion section *"Have an Idea? Let's Build It."*
12. **Contact Form**: Pre-selectable service pills, direct input, instant feedback, and email integration.
13. **Footer**: Brand badges, nav links, social links, and copyright info.
